# Snooze Button Roast

A one-line roast of an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, for the make.fun
"Snooze Button Roast" bounty.

## The line

> My alarm went off at 6 AM on a Saturday — the only employee in this house who shows up early on its day off, and it doesn't even get paid.

138 characters, one line. Concrete scene (the alarm as an overeager employee) with the
twist in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and checks the entry gate first
(1–200 characters, single line, not blank). Run it with `node main.ts`.
